Questions

Before you book.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome when a barber is available. Booking ahead is the best way to lock in your preferred time.

How long does a haircut take?

Most standard cuts take 30–45 minutes. Skin fades and combination services may take a little longer.

Can I request a specific barber?

Yes. Mention the barber in the notes section of your booking request and we’ll do our best to accommodate it.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Cash and major credit/debit cards are accepted in the shop.
